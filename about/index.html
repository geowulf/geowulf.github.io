About

Greg Woolf tells stories for a living — he just hasn't always called it that.

By day, Greg is a software developer. By early morning, he's a rideshare driver. On weekends, he's a certified motorcycle rider coach with the Harley-Davidson Riding Academy. In every one of those seats, people tell him their stories — and he's been collecting them for years.

The Wayteller is his project to give those stories a home: a blog and a podcast about how people's choices are shaped by the ways they were taught — and how those ways tell the story of their actions and behaviors.

He lives in Chandler, Arizona, with his wife Jen and their two kids. He believes life is a story, and the telling of it matters.

The philosophy underneath
